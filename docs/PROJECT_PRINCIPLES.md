# Performance Coach Platform

## Project Principles

**Version:** 1.0

**Status:** Draft

**Author:** Bryan Esser

**Last Updated:** 25 July 2026

---

## Purpose

This document defines the engineering and architectural principles that guide the Performance Coach Platform.

Every architectural decision, feature, API, database schema, and AI capability should align with these principles.

---

## Vision

Build the world's most intelligent, provider-agnostic Performance Intelligence Platform that empowers athletes and coaches to make better decisions through trusted, explainable AI.

---

## Mission

Enable athletes and coaches to make better decisions by transforming performance data into trusted, explainable, and actionable coaching intelligence.

---

# Core Values

## 1. Athlete First

Every feature should improve outcomes for athletes and coaches.

Technology is not the product.

Better coaching decisions are the product.

---

## 2. AI Native

Artificial Intelligence is a core capability of the platform, not an optional feature.

Every new domain should consider how its data can contribute to future AI reasoning, coaching recommendations, and performance insights.

---

## 3. Provider Agnostic

The platform must not depend on any single wearable provider or fitness ecosystem.

External providers such as Garmin, Apple Health, Google Health Connect, COROS, Polar, Suunto, Strava, and future integrations are treated as data sources rather than business domains.

Business logic operates only on the platform's normalized data models.

---

## 4. Evidence Based

Recommendations generated by the platform should be supported by measurable evidence wherever practical.

The platform should distinguish between:

- Measured data
- Derived metrics
- AI-generated recommendations

Users should be able to understand why a recommendation was made.
